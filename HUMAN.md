# HUMAN.md — what the person in this loop actually did

In my own words. Short on purpose.

I never wanted to do homework. Teachers said "show your work" and I found ways around it. Thirty years later I fix appliances for a living and I build AI systems on the side, and the honest reason is that my mind never wanted to do the physical work of research, the reading, the sitting with a thing until I understood it. So I built machines that do that part. This folder is me showing my work on that fact. Building the system is real knowing-how; I'll defend that anywhere. Knowing Clifford, or knowing what happened in India in 2018, is a different job, and the machines drafting it doesn't mean I did it.
