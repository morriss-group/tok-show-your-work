## What changed because of this review

- **Forwarders line:** "Almost none… thought of themselves as pulling a trigger" (a claim about strangers' minds) became "Nobody who forwarded them had to mean any harm," which is the actual point.
- **The attackable pair:** the first paragraph now says the second-model check "is a check between machines, which… is not the same as a check against the world." The tension is inside the argument instead of hiding under it.
- **1996:** "If the book wasn't there, the answer wasn't there" became "If the library didn't have it, you waited weeks or went without," which is what interlibrary loan actually felt like.
- **Teacher line:** cut to one sentence of thanks; the "outsider" phrasing is gone.
- **"because I thought it mattered," "most people in this thread don't have," "AI that books real jobs," "A lot of you are being told," "the jobs you're planning for":** all softened or removed as flagged. "Run my own shop on" became "try to run my own shop on."
- **Disclosure:** now says a fresh Grok chat reviewed the final draft; the students' note describes the pipeline as "the same tools described under my answer" rather than compressing it.
- **Names:** the students' note now names the two executives so the claims can be checked, with "within days of each other" instead of "the same week."

## What was kept, and why

- The lynching case stays. Grok calls it TOK-legal but heavy; it is the one verified harm case with a body count, and the argument needs one.
- The teacher thanks stays in one sentence. The author asked for it, and a sentence is not flattery.
- "About two dozen" stays as is, per the review.
