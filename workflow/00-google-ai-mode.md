As knowers, do we have a moral responsibility to verify information before sharing it in digital spaces?

**Google AI Mode answer (verbatim):**

Yes, knowers bear a distinct moral responsibility to verify information before sharing it digitally. In the framework of Theory of Knowledge (TOK), sharing unverified data alters the shared knowledge landscape, potentially causing real-world harm.

Brief Rationale

Epistemic Duty: Sharing information makes you a source of authority. Knowers must uphold the integrity of the shared knowledge pool.

Preventing Harm: Misinformation can influence elections, damage public health, and manipulate financial markets.

The Echo Chamber Effect: Digital algorithms reward sensationalism over truth. Unverified shares accelerate the spread of false frameworks.

Undermining Trust: Proliferating errors erodes institutional trust. This makes it harder for communities to agree on foundational facts.
